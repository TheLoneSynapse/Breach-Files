# Group A — videos / laws / punishment (verified by researcher, IDs pending curl check)

Kevin Mitnick
- video: aUqes9QdLQ4 :: "My Adventures as the World's Most Wanted Hacker | Kevin Mitnick | Talks at Google" :: Talks at Google :: talk/interview
- laws: 1999 plea — four counts wire fraud (18 U.S.C. 1343), two counts computer fraud (18 U.S.C. 1030), one count illegally intercepting a wire communication (18 U.S.C. 2511). 1995 arrest was for violating probation.
- punishment: 46 months (1999 case), three years supervised release, $4,125 restitution; incarcerated from 1995, released 2000.

Robert Tappan Morris
- video: 2QwMv0_Rkec :: "The First Internet Worm (Morris Worm) - Computerphile" :: Computerphile :: explainer interview
- laws: convicted after jury trial of one CFAA count, 18 U.S.C. 1030(a)(5)(A) — accessing a federal-interest computer without authorization and causing damage/loss.
- punishment: three years' probation, 400 hours community service, $10,050 fine plus supervision costs; no prison.

Adrian Lamo
- video: uFVIu2g7uzQ :: "Adrian Lamo, Bradley Manning Informant, Defends Turning in Alleged WikiLeaks Whistleblower" :: Democracy Now! :: interview
- laws: pleaded guilty to one count of unauthorized access/damage to the New York Times network (computer damage offense under the CFAA).
- punishment: two years' probation including six months home detention, approximately $65,000 restitution.

Aaron Swartz
- video: 0MUjdc3KIYA :: "The Story of Aaron Swartz: \"The Internet's Own Boy\" Director Q&A" :: documentary filmmaker Q&A
- laws: superseding federal indictment — 13 felonies: two wire fraud (18 U.S.C. 1343) and 11 CFAA counts (18 U.S.C. 1030). Allegations only.
- punishment: no sentence or conviction; case pending when he died January 2013, then dismissed.

Edward Snowden
- video: 0hLjuVyIIrs :: "NSA whistleblower Edward Snowden: 'I don't want to live in a society that does these sort of things'" :: The Guardian :: interview
- laws: 2013 complaint — theft of government property (18 U.S.C. 641), unauthorized communication of national-defense information (18 U.S.C. 793(d)), willful communication of classified communications-intelligence information (18 U.S.C. 798(a)(3)). Pending charges, not convictions.
- punishment: no US sentence; outside US custody, granted Russian citizenship 2022.

Chelsea Manning
- video: gpgKa6fBo4Y :: "Chelsea Manning interview on Trump, running for office, and prison life" :: Channel 4 News :: interview
- laws: court-martial under the UCMJ — aiding the enemy (Article 104, acquitted), Espionage Act offences prosecuted under Article 134, theft and computer-related offences; convicted on 20 charges.
- punishment: 35 years, dishonorable discharge, reduction to Private E-1; commuted by President Obama, released 2017 after about seven years.

Julian Assange
- video: hUKGlriwhjU :: "\"I Pled Guilty to Journalism\": WikiLeaks' Julian Assange Speaks Publicly After Prison Release" :: Democracy Now! :: news footage
- laws: US — pleaded guilty to one count of conspiracy to obtain, receive and disclose national-defense information, 18 U.S.C. 793(g) (Espionage Act). UK — convicted of breaching bail conditions.
- punishment: US sentence of 62 months credited as time served, released June 2024; separately 50 weeks in UK prison for bail breach.

Kevin Poulsen
- video: SKVnR91ed5g :: "DEFCON 20: Kevin Poulsen Answers Your Questions" :: conference Q&A
- laws: pleaded guilty to seven counts — computer fraud, interception of wire communications, mail fraud, money laundering, obstruction of justice (radio-contest scheme).
- punishment: 51 months in prison plus $57,925 restitution to three radio stations.

Gary McKinnon
- video: XacevWeOkHg :: "Gary McKinnon Interview - BBC" :: interview
- laws: US indictment alleged seven computer-crime counts under 18 U.S.C. 1030; UK Crown Prosecution Service brought no charges.
- punishment: no conviction or sentence; extradition blocked in 2012 on human-rights/medical grounds, UK declined to charge.

George Hotz (GeoHot)
- video: Qk5ZoLizNgE :: "Sony vs. Geohot: The Hacking Battle" :: news/commentary
- laws: no criminal charge. Sony brought a civil suit including DMCA-related allegations over his PS3 publication.
- punishment: civil settlement plus permanent injunction; no criminal penalty, no disclosed payment.

Dan Kaminsky
- video: 7Pp72gUYx00 :: "DEF CON 16 - Dan Kaminsky: Black Ops 2008" :: DEF CON talk
- laws: none — no charge or conviction for the 2008 DNS vulnerability; coordinated disclosure instead.
- punishment: none.

John Draper (Captain Crunch)
- video: n6TjjE8NHV0 :: "Phone Phreak Captain Crunch Rare Interview from '78" :: interview
- laws: 1972 arrest on seven wire-fraud counts, 18 U.S.C. 1343; separate 1976 wire-fraud conviction. (Two different proceedings — do not merge.)
- punishment: 1972 — suspended one-year sentence, $1,000 fine, five years probation; 1976 — four months in federal prison.
